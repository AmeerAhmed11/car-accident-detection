## Scene 1: The Crisis
- **Frames:** 0 – 330
- **Duration:** 331 frames (~11.03 seconds)
- **Visuals:** 
  The video begins with a dark, moody background featuring a subtle, pulsing red glow to establish a sense of urgency. High-impact text statistics slide in dramatically, one by one:
  1. **11,500** Traffic Accidents Annually (Appears at frame 45)
  2. **3,000** Fatalities on National Roads
  3. **5–10 Min** Traditional Notification Delay
  
  Towards the end of the scene, a striking red glassmorphism card slides up from the bottom. It features an animated progress bar and a rapidly ticking digital counter that stops at **+2.6%**. The label reads: "Fatality Rate Increase per minute of delay".
- **Narrative Purpose:** To immediately hook the audience by highlighting the deadly cost of delayed emergency response times.

## Scene 4: Triple-Verification Pipeline
- **Frames:** 861 – 1160
- **Duration:** 300 frames (~10.00 seconds)
- **Visuals:** 
  The camera dynamically pans and zooms across a sleek grid of three animated algorithms running in parallel:
  1. **Vector Direction Check:** Two colored lines intersect, verifying the physical collision of vehicle trajectories.
  2. **Velocity Drop Analysis:** An animated bar chart drops sharply to zero, showing sudden vehicular stops.
  3. **Frame Persistence:** A progress bar fills up to 30/30 frames to ensure the detection isn't a glitch.
  
  All three panels start with a gray "ANALYZING..." tag, which flips to a glowing green "VERIFIED". Finally, a massive green badge drops in: **"STATUS: ALARM VALIDATED"**.
- **Narrative Purpose:** To prove to technical judges that the AI is highly sophisticated and heavily filters out false alarms.

- **Frames:** 1461 – 1890
- **Duration:** 429 frames (~14.30 seconds)
- **Visuals:**

  The video holds on this success state so the judges can clearly read the bounding boxes.
  
  **At Frame 1825 (Exactly 2.1 seconds before the end):** 
  A massive, unmissable frosted-glass overlay drops down covering the screen. It reads:
  **"TEST THE MODEL YOURSELF"**
  And displays the live web URL in bold glowing green: **car-accident-detection.vercel.app**
  The video abruptly cuts to black at exactly 63.0 seconds.
- **Narrative Purpose:** To provide the ultimate proof—showing the live app working, and ending with a powerful mic-drop moment inviting the judges to test the link themselves.
